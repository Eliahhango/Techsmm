Understanding YouTube Monetization Policy on Fake Views and Subscribers

One of the biggest concerns for monetized creators is compliance.

The YouTube monetization policy on fake views and subscribers is designed to protect advertisers, viewers, and creators who build audiences honestly.

YouTube actively monitors unusual activity such as:

  • Invalid traffic
  • Artificial engagement
  • Purchased fake subscribers
  • Click fraud
  • Automated viewing behavior

If these activities are detected, YouTube may:

  • Remove invalid views
  • Remove fake subscribers
  • Limit monetization
  • Suspend advertising
  • Remove channels from the YouTube Partner Program in severe cases

For this reason, creators should never rely on services that promise unrealistic growth overnight.

Instead, promotion should always support—not replace—real audience building.
